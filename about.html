PROJECT VISION

🌤️ MAUSAM

Personalized. Predictive. Proactive.

MAUSAM provides the right weather information to the right person at the right time.

Our Goal

Different users need different weather information. A farmer needs rainfall information, a traveller needs destination weather, a commuter needs rain and road conditions, while a parent may need school travel conditions.

MAUSAM Features

Personalized weather dashboards, intelligent alerts, predictive insights, hyperlocal information, weather-aware routes and AI-powered assistance.
